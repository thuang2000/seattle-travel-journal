Week 02 Design System

The Cascadia Journal

A warm editorial design system for a travel publication exploring Seattle and the surrounding Pacific Northwest.

01

Perceptual Color

The palette uses OKLCH so that lightness and color intensity can be adjusted more predictably than with traditional hexadecimal colors.

Primary

oklch(51% 0.12 42)

A warm terracotta color used for links, highlights, and editorial accents.

Secondary

oklch(42% 0.07 145)

A subdued evergreen inspired by the forests surrounding the Puget Sound.

Background

oklch(97% 0.02 85)

A warm paper-like background for the light color scheme.

Text

oklch(25% 0.035 45)

A dark warm brown that provides strong contrast against the light background.

Contrast Demonstration

This text uses the main text color against the main background color. The large lightness difference creates clear, readable contrast.

Approximate light-mode contrast ratio: 14.8:1

02

Fluid Typography

The typography grows smoothly as the viewport becomes wider while maintaining accessible minimum and maximum font sizes.

--size-heading-lg

Journeys through the Pacific Northwest

clamp(1.75rem, 1.31rem + 1.88vw, 3rem)

--size-heading-md

Weekend destinations near Seattle

clamp(1.35rem, 1.16rem + 0.82vw, 2rem)

--size-base

From quiet island communities to mountain trails, this journal collects stories from destinations throughout the region.

1rem

03

Spacing Constants

A shared spacing scale creates consistent gaps, padding, and margins throughout the publication.

Extra Small

--space-xs: 0.5rem

Small

--space-sm: 0.75rem

Medium

--space-md: 1.5rem

Large

--space-lg: 3rem
